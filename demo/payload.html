How to play

Build a scoring machine. Place drafted parts on the grid, then drop marbles down a column — every part they touch mutates their value. Adds feed multipliers, prisms clone marbles, springs re-trigger what's above. Beat the quota; it climbs steeply. Multiplication or death.
